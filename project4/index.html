Analyst interpretation

Decision: Compare hourly and weekday pickup volumes to identify periods with greater observed trip activity. Use the pickup-zone ranking to prioritize locations for further service-coverage investigation.

Validation: The data-quality audit reports problematic records separately. Trip-duration metrics use a documented 1–180-minute filter rather than silently including impossible or extreme values.

Next investigation: Join the official taxi-zone lookup, compare multiple months, and quantify variability before recommending staffing levels or predicting demand.

Data lineage & limitations

Source: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission. Monthly Yellow Taxi Parquet files are queried using DuckDB. This dashboard displays precomputed, versionable SQL output rather than loading millions of rows into a visitor's browser.

Pickup volume is observed trip activity, not unserved demand. These records do not measure available vehicles, driver shifts, or dispatch response times. Data-quality exceptions are reported separately and are not silently discarded.
