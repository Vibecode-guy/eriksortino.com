Data lineage & limitations

Source: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission. Monthly Yellow Taxi Parquet files are queried using DuckDB. This dashboard displays precomputed, versionable SQL output rather than loading millions of rows into a visitor's browser.

Pickup volume is observed trip activity, not unserved demand. These records do not measure available vehicles, driver shifts, or dispatch response times. Data-quality exceptions are reported separately and are not silently discarded.
