PROJECT 03 — EV NETWORK EXPANSION STRATEGY

DATA SOURCE
U.S. Department of Energy Alternative Fuels Data Center (AFDC), Alternative Fueling Station Locator and Alternative Fuel Corridor station datasets.

SOURCE FIELDS USED / PLANNED
Station status and access; EV network; Level 1, Level 2 and DC-fast EVSE port counts; connector types; latitude/longitude; city/state/ZIP; open date; date last confirmed; updated timestamp; facility type; funding sources when present.

TRANSFORMATIONS
1. Filter to electric stations and the intended access/status scope.
2. Normalize null port counts and network naming.
3. Calculate total ports, DC-fast share and ports per station.
4. Aggregate by market/state/network/facility type.
5. Keep federal corridor-qualified stations as a distinct strategic layer.
6. Normalize strategy factors to 0–100.
7. Apply transparent portfolio weights to build an Expansion Priority Score.
8. Convert rankings into Expand / Densify / Defer recommendations.

PORTFOLIO SCORE — NOT A DOE METRIC
35% Coverage Gap + 30% DC-Fast Gap + 20% Corridor Relevance + 15% Network White Space.

LIMITATIONS
A real capital decision would require additional demand/economic inputs such as EV registrations, traffic/AADT, utilization, electricity/interconnection costs, real estate, permitting, incentives, competitive pipeline and expected returns.
